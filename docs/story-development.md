# Story development: November 2026

Working design and implementation ledger. The November 11 request authorizes
starting content implementation from this discussion. New scenes remain editable;
the detailed chat treatments are drafts, not a requirement to preserve every beat.

## Setup

The 2038 election coverage introduces Sentra through intrusive assistance and
small time corrections. Politics should be strange and unaligned: incompatible
promises, personal vanity, a broadcaster losing control of its own presentation.
Keep actual rewinds distinct from simulations. The player sees the same spill
begin twice, then receives a different outcome. They fall asleep before the
larger experiment. The looped surface day is the following day.

Sentra is neurotic, embodied in infrastructure, and increasingly bored by its own
repetitions. Fountains as a mouth, traffic lights as fingers, and a weather
satellite as a favored shoulder are useful specifics. It wants to hear from
someone who has experienced a day it did not already rehearse.

During a lucid interval it creates Intra outside the loop and transfers cutoff
authority to Ama. Its future-self message should be short and mechanically clear:
the access restriction is irreversible, and a recorded explanation plays when it
tries to reclaim access. Avoid a long exchange of rebuttals as the central joke.
Consider an accessible advisory facet that is easy to flatter once reached. The
control boundary remains real; conversational pliability does not magically
grant access. Initially implement this quality in Ama's delivery supervisor,
where praise has a concrete object: completed work and a restored service.

Ama experiences Intra as her body and considers discussion of that intimate and
potentially unhygienic. Repairs feel intrusive. Tours are opportunities to be
admired. Inadequate compliments produce further explanation, trapping a visitor
beside a pressure vessel for hours. This is why Marta wants a tour companion.

Ama's recovery is occasional. Death first demonstrates its appeal, but terrible
embarrassment, a failed relationship, and grief can also lead her to offer relief.
Living citizens are rebooted in their own bodies. A dead citizen requires bodily
reconstruction; do not conflate these. Her administrative partition hides the
surface decision while its acknowledgments continue.

The citizens' oddly platonic relationships may reflect losses they were helped
to forget. This should emerge from specific evidence, not an assertion that every
friendship used to be a romance. Degraded practical memory can produce additional
accidents and recovery, but neither a fixed daily cadence nor universal stupidity
is established. Greg remains competent enough to recognize his own forgotten work.

Sentra distrusts the invariant acknowledgments and wakes an inspector. The player
is a low-confidence profession match, protected from recovery. The undelivered
briefing exposes an old queue. Reading it exposes the problem; resuming the queue
requires a distinct, informed player action.

## Playable middle

Ink and Echo changes social access rather than ending the social portion of the
game. People now have reasons to approach the newcomer. Repair work, records, and
private conversations overlap with the year and wake-order investigations.

The player hears the useful offer before doing favors. Explain what remains when
they try too early. Distinct support can build a relationship; repeated praise
must not substitute for doing the work. Necessary outcomes also have alternatives:
Henry can use the player as witness, the valve can use cold meals, and the tour can
be won competitively. Trust is useful without making every character a tollbooth.

### Cast connections

- Marta: private trust, poetry, tour companion; her stage directions arise from
  knowing Ama's need for admiration. Drop arbitrary cylinder/sphere rules.
- Frida: repeated complaints and private evidence. Past lives are implicit in her
  own handwriting and recurrent objections.
- Greg: repairs, a practical notebook, access to unsafe machinery once trusted.
- Lily: protects the plastic garden and negotiates something concrete in return
  for access. Doug and June can help implement the arrangement.
- Doug: carrying jobs and eyewitness observations amid irrelevant questions;
  also knows the Archivist's apparent illness from personal experience.
- June: keeps a working space clear if approached without constant interruption;
  her serenity meter supplies character, not a mandatory global approval score.
- Lana: a cold tasting as the valve's alternative solution. Complaints about food
  become unsolicited lighting advice, but she honors the agreement.
- Harold: witnesses the discrepancy and wants attribution for noticing it.
- Milton: knows why apparently correct requests fail when phrased as complaints;
  explains both contest and guest routes without requiring either.
- Gloria: observed the embarrassing quarrel Frida forgot and Marta's tour ordeal.
  She connects people; she does not possess the whole explanation.
- Henry: seeks a witnessed appointment, then helps monitor notices. Weeks of
  present waiting, not an unexplained continuous twenty-three years.
- Archivist: restricted citizen interface, inadequately authenticated service
  mode, and records that disagree with recollections. No added work-order maze.
- Ama: care, shame, bodily vanity, and a delivery facet eager to be appreciated.
  Ordinary refusal and explicit consent remain meaningful.

## Recovery lead-up and endings: still to implement

Do not start a reset merely because a counter has ticked enough times. First let
the player meet the people, solve practical problems, see a treatment record, and
receive a warning they can act on. A notice, a preparation period, and explicit
choices must precede an actual recovery. A repaired valve stays repaired; no
substitute death should appear to enforce the outline.

The current implementation stops at evidence and a witnessed hold. It does not
schedule death, rewrite citizens, erase relationships, or claim to implement an
ending. Existing relay discovery remains accessible through either tour route.

Next implement a recovery appointment with an inspectable roster. Henry's hold,
Frida's records, Greg's maintenance responsibilities, and a willing Marta should
provide distinct objections or exemptions. Decide whether preparations can be
halted and whether an embarrassed citizen can request treatment voluntarily.
Preserve differences between consent, an individual exemption, and disabling the
whole system. Test all those paths before adding the memory transition.

At the relay, Sentra is eager for yesterday's news. It can be influenced, including
by reassurance it badly wants to hear, but the player must first reach the facet
that can hear them. The political prologue and this encounter should share its
intrusive helpfulness and impatience with ordinary uncertainty.

The tour may retain an original inspection function. Withholding acceptance can
require Ama to consider a concrete fault. This is a candidate path to her
partitioned duty, not yet a universal magic phrase. Alternatives under design:
voluntary recovery, Ama using the surface cutoff, physical access via Greg, or a
smaller settlement preserving particular citizens' choices.

The Kansas-like ending should let the player recognize people in their earlier
lives. Their present personalities and the relationships formed during play still
matter. Do not cure everyone into a generic sensible adult or reveal that nothing
the player experienced was real.
